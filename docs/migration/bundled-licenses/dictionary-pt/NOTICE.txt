Brazilian Portuguese spelling dictionary (VERO)

Leafloom distributes the dictionary and affix data from dictionary-pt 4.0.0,
which is VERO, the Brazilian Portuguese Hunspell dictionary used by
LibreOffice, under the Mozilla Public License 2.0 option of its
LGPL-3.0 / MPL license. Leafloom's MIT license does not replace the
dictionary's license, and Leafloom does not remove the LGPL alternative.

Source availability

The unmodified dictionary source files index.aff and index.dic and upstream
license accompany the bundled dictionary package. Packaging retains this
notice and the selected license beside the third-party license collection.

On macOS, relative to Leafloom.app/Contents/Resources:
  host/node_modules/dictionary-pt/index.aff
  host/node_modules/dictionary-pt/index.dic
  host/licenses/dictionary-pt/NOTICE.txt

The same files are available from the pinned source package:
https://registry.npmjs.org/dictionary-pt/-/dictionary-pt-4.0.0.tgz
