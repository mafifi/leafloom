# Writing

Choose the reader's voice.

| Voice | Material | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Publishing | Website, articles, product copy | Clear, simple and confident. Explain the product and why it matters. Keep internal records, test results and caveats elsewhere. |
| Developer | README, architecture, contributing, guides | Condensed and purposeful. Name boundaries, commands and responsibilities. Link to authoritative details. |
| Agent | AGENTS, plans, working notes, skills | Record evidence, unresolved decisions, failures and exact next actions. |

Use familiar words and active verbs. Put the concrete point first. Avoid inflated language, reflexive contrasts, stacked caveats and comments about the writing itself. Keep one authoritative home for each fact; link instead of repeating it.
